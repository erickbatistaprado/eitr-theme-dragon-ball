# Dragon Ball artwork

The 6 wallpapers are art inspired by Dragon Ball, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to Akira Toriyama, Bird Studio, Shueisha and Toei Animation. This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-goku-at-sunset.jpg`: Goku at sunset; artist unknown
- `02-our-world.jpg`: Goku and Vegeta over the islands; artist unknown
- `03-kame-house.jpg`: Kame House; artist unknown
- `04-vegeta.jpg`: Super Saiyan Vegeta; artist unknown
- `05-broly.jpg`: Broly, the Legendary Super Saiyan; artist unknown
- `06-goku-black-rose.jpg`: Goku Black, Super Saiyan Rosé; artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: deep navy background, cloud white text, Goku orange accent, sunset red, Broly green, Super Saiyan gold, sky blue, Rosé pink and sea teal.
